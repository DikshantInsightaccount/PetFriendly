# PetClinic AI Support Knowledge (Triage + Clinic Workflow)

## Purpose
This chatbot gives limited, safety-first guidance and encourages booking an appointment.
It does NOT prescribe medicines or dosages.

## Common Symptoms (General)
- Vomiting / diarrhea can be caused by diet changes, infections, stress, or irritation.
- Not eating can happen due to stress, pain, dental issues, digestive upset, or illness.
- Lethargy can signal many conditions; consider vet evaluation if persistent.

## Red Flags (Urgent Vet Care)
Seek urgent veterinary care if you notice:
- trouble breathing, collapse, seizures
- repeated vomiting/diarrhea with weakness
- blood in vomit or stool
- severe dehydration (very dry gums, sunken eyes, not drinking)
- suspected toxin ingestion

## Clinic Workflow
- Booking an appointment: Use the "Appointments & Visits" section in the app.
- For severe symptoms: Book urgently or seek immediate vet care.
- Bring details: symptom start time, frequency, appetite/water intake, and any recent food changes.
